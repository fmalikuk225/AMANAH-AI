# AMANAH AI Reliability Boundary

## What CHECK does
- Detects whether a specific, potentially traceable citation or URL is visible.
- Separates a vague source mention (for example “Quran”, “hadith”, “scholars”) from a traceable citation.
- Flags sweeping certainty and sharing-pressure language.
- States when evidence is insufficient.

## What CHECK does not do
- It does not issue fatwas.
- It does not label arbitrary Islamic claims true/false.
- It does not treat a citation as proof that an interpretation is correct.
- It does not claim scholar review unless such review has actually happened.

## Safety principle
When evidence is insufficient, the safe output is uncertainty plus a recommendation to verify the original source or seek appropriate qualified guidance.
