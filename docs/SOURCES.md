# AMANAH AI — Sources & Provenance

## Religious / institutional evidence used in the MVP

1. **Quran 49:6 — Al-Hujurat**  
   URL: https://quran.com/49/6  
   Use: Supports the learning principle of verifying consequential information before acting or amplifying it.  
   Status: Traceable Quranic reference.  
   Note: AMANAH does not extrapolate a new religious ruling from the verse.

2. **Egypt's Dar Al-Ifta — Using AI applications to obtain fatwas**  
   URL: https://www.dar-alifta.org/en/fatwa/details/22255/using-ai-applications-to-obtain-fatwas  
   Date shown by source: 2 December 2025.  
   Use: Evidence for scenarios teaching caution around treating general AI output as qualified religious guidance.  
   Status: Official institutional source.

3. **Egypt's Dar Al-Ifta — Resolving Family Issues: The Dilemma of AI-Generated Fatwas**  
   URL: https://www.dar-alifta.org/en/article/details/12325/resolving-family-issues-the-dilemma-of-ai-generated-fatwas  
   Use: Evidence for the distinction between AI-generated information and context-sensitive qualified guidance.  
   Status: Official institutional source.

## Scenario provenance
All five scenario narratives in the MVP are **synthetic, controlled educational scenarios authored for AMANAH AI**. They do not portray a real living scholar and do not use fabricated hadith numbers or fabricated Quranic references. Their labels such as “AI chat”, “social post”, “short video”, and “forwarded audio” describe the simulated digital context, not actual media captured from a third party.

## Scoring provenance
All dashboard values are calculated only from the user's current session. No population benchmark, clinical claim, user-study result, or research-effectiveness percentage is embedded in the MVP.
